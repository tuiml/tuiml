About TuiML

TuiML is the agent-native ML runtime: classical machine learning that AI agents can actually call. It is developed by a team of researchers and engineers at the AI Institute, University of Waikato. Our approach combines research insights with practical development efforts, ensuring the project maintains academic rigor while also being applicable in real-world scenarios.

Core maintainers

Active developers who oversee discussions, manage releases, ensure module integration, and handle coding tasks in general.
